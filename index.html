How to figure out how much material you need

Short answer: multiply length × width in feet by depth in inches, divide by 324 to get cubic yards, and add about 10% extra. Then compare the bag count at the store with a bulk load plus its delivery fee. Small jobs are cheaper in bags; big ones are cheaper in bulk.

The formula

Cubic feet = length (ft) × width (ft) × depth (in) ÷ 12 Cubic yards = cubic feet ÷ 27 Tons = cubic yards × density (gravel ≈ 1.4, sand ≈ 1.35, topsoil ≈ 1.1) Bags = cubic feet ÷ bag size (80 lb concrete = 0.6 ft³), rounded up Bulk cost = amount ordered × price + delivery (+ short-load fee for ready-mix)

Worked example: a 10 × 10 ft patio, 4 inches thick

StepResult
10 × 10 × 4 ÷ 1233.3 ft³
+10% extra36.7 ft³ = 1.36 yd³
80 lb bags (36.7 ÷ 0.6)62 bags = $403 at $6.50
Ready-mix (1.5 yd³ × $170 + $150 short load)$405

At this size it's a toss-up on price, but the truck saves mixing 62 bags (almost 5,000 lb). One size smaller and bags win; one size bigger and the truck wins. That's why every BagOrBulk calculator shows both.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate cubic yards?

Multiply length (ft) by width (ft) by depth (in), divide by 12 to get cubic feet, then divide by 27. Shortcut: square feet × depth in inches ÷ 324 = cubic yards.

How many bags of concrete are in a yard?

About 45 bags of 80 lb concrete, 60 bags of 60 lb or 90 bags of 40 lb, using the manufacturers' yields of 0.6, 0.45 and 0.3 cubic feet per bag.

How much does a yard of gravel weigh?

About 1.4 tons (2,800 lb). It ranges from 1.3 to 1.5 tons depending on stone size and moisture.

How many bags of mulch are in a yard?

13.5 bags of 2 cubic feet, or 9 bags of 3 cubic feet.

When is bulk cheaper than bags?

At typical 2026 prices, bulk wins above about half a cubic yard of gravel, about 1 yard of topsoil, 1¼ yards of concrete and 5 yards of mulch. Delivery fees and local prices move these points, so enter yours.

How much extra should I buy?

About 10% for concrete, gravel and soil (spillage, compaction and settling) and 5% for mulch. The calculators add it automatically, and you can change it.
