How much mulch for a 5×10 bed?

A 5 × 10 ft bed is typical for a medium garden bed. Bags and yards at 2, 3 and 4 inches deep.

A 5 × 10 ft bed (50 sq ft) mulched 3 inches deep needs 0.49 cubic yards or 7 bags of 2 cubic feet (5 bags of 3 ft³), including 5% extra. A 2 inch layer takes 5 bags; a 4 inch layer 9.

Worked example: 3 inches deep

At 3 inches, bags win: 7 bags cost about $28 versus $115 for a delivered 1.0 yd³ (which includes a $75 delivery fee).

Prices used: 2 ft³ bag $4, 3 ft³ bag $6; bulk mulch $40/yd³ plus $75 delivery (1 yd³ minimum). Spring sales change the crossover.

Top-ups and depth

If the bed already has mulch, a 1 inch refresh takes only 0.16 yd³ or 3 bags of 2 ft³. Keep mulch 2–3 inches away from trunks and stems.

FAQ

How many bags of mulch for a 5x10 bed?

7 bags of 2 cubic feet at 3 inches deep (5 at 2 inches, 9 at 4 inches), each with 5% extra.

How many cubic yards of mulch for a 5x10 bed?

0.49 cubic yards at 3 inches; 0.32 at 2 inches; 0.65 at 4 inches.

Is bagged or bulk mulch cheaper for a 5x10 bed?

At typical prices bags is cheaper: $28 in bags versus $115 delivered.
