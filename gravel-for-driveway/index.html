How much gravel for a driveway?

Tons for common driveway sizes, at a top-up depth and a full new-driveway depth.

A 50 × 10 ft driveway at 4 inches needs about 9.5 tons of gravel with 10% extra; at 6 inches it needs 14.3 tons. For a new driveway, plan 6–8 inches in layers: large base stone, then #57 stone, then a finer top layer.

Layering a new gravel driveway

  1. Base (4 in): #3 or #4 stone (1.5–3 in), compacted.
  2. Middle (2–3 in): #57 stone (¾–1 in).
  3. Top (2 in): crusher run or #411 (stone plus fines) that packs hard.

Add a woven geotextile fabric on soft soil. Add one gravel calculator area per layer, each with its own depth.
