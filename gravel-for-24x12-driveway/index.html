How much gravel for a 24×12 driveway?

A 24 × 12 ft area is typical for a one-car driveway or a short turnaround. Tons and yards at five depths.

A 24 × 12 ft driveway at 4 inches deep needs about 5.5 tons (3.9 cubic yards) of gravel with 10% extra. At 6 inches it needs 8.2 tons (5.9 yd³). Delivered in bulk at typical prices, 4 inches costs about $348.

At 4 inches, a bulk delivery is cheaper here ($1,166 in bags versus $348 delivered). Bagged gravel weighs about 10,600 lb in total for this size, so bulk is the practical way to buy for a driveway.

Prices used: bulk gravel $45 per ton plus $100 delivery (1 ton minimum); 0.5 ft³ bags $5.50. Quarry prices vary widely by stone type and region.

Layering a 24 × 12 ft driveway

A new gravel driveway is built in layers. Using a 4 in base of large stone and a 2 in top of crusher run (6 in total) takes about 5.5 tons for the base and 2.7 tons for the top, 8.2 tons overall. To top up an existing driveway you usually need only 2–3 inches: 2.7–4.1 tons here.

Add a woven geotextile fabric on soft soil, and crown the center so water sheds. Order 10–20% extra for compaction on a new base.

FAQ

How many tons of gravel for a 24x12 driveway?

About 5.5 tons at 4 inches, 8.2 tons at 6 inches and 10.9 tons at 8 inches, each with 10% extra.

How many cubic yards of gravel is a 24x12 area?

3.91 cubic yards at 4 inches deep and 1.96 at 2 inches, with 10% extra.

How much does gravel for a 24x12 driveway cost?

About $348 delivered at 4 inches ($45 per ton plus a $100 delivery fee), versus $1,166 in 0.5 ft³ bags.
