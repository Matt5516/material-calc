# BagOrBulk

Free concrete, gravel, mulch and topsoil calculators. Get cubic yards, tons and bag counts, and see whether bags or a bulk delivery costs less.

**Live site: [https://bagorbulk.com/](https://bagorbulk.com/)** (free, no signup, runs in your browser)

## Tools
- [Concrete calculator](https://bagorbulk.com/concrete-calculator/): Slabs, round pads and post holes: yards, 40/60/80 lb bags, and bags vs a ready-mix truck.
- [Gravel calculator](https://bagorbulk.com/gravel-calculator/): Tons and cubic yards for driveways, paths and patios, plus bags vs a delivered load.
- [Mulch calculator](https://bagorbulk.com/mulch-calculator/): How many bags or yards of mulch for your beds, and which is cheaper.
- [Topsoil & sand calculator](https://bagorbulk.com/topsoil-calculator/): Raised beds, lawns and paver sand: cubic yards, bags and bulk cost.
- [Cubic yard calculator](https://bagorbulk.com/cubic-yard-calculator/): Any material: cubic feet, cubic yards, cubic meters and tons, with your own density.
- [Grass seed calculator](https://bagorbulk.com/grass-seed-calculator/): Pounds and bags of grass seed for a new lawn or overseeding, by grass type.
- [Firewood calculator](https://bagorbulk.com/firewood-calculator/): Cords, face cords and store bundles from a stack size, plus bundles vs a delivered cord.
- [Paver calculator](https://bagorbulk.com/paver-calculator/): How many pavers for a patio, plus the gravel base and bedding sand.
- [Sand calculator](https://bagorbulk.com/sand-calculator/): Cubic yards, tons and 50 lb bags of sand for pavers, play areas and leveling.
- [Ice melt calculator](https://bagorbulk.com/ice-melt-calculator/): Bags of ice melt per application and for the winter, plus bag vs bulk or pallet cost.

## Data sources
Conversion formulas are standard geometry (1 cubic yard = 27 cubic feet). Bag yields and densities come from typical manufacturer figures for concrete, gravel, mulch and topsoil, and default prices are typical 2026 US figures that you can edit. Real products and local prices vary, so check with your supplier.

## About this repo
Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript on GitHub Pages. No ads, no cookies. The math lives in `assets/calc.js`. Corrections: [open an issue](https://github.com/Matt5516/material-calc/issues).

Estimates only. Bag yields, densities and prices vary by product and region, so check with your supplier before you order. Not affiliated with any store, quarry or concrete plant.
